Our Strategic Action Plan

Aligned and Integrated with our WASC Accreditation

Our 2024 Western Association of Schools and Colleges (WASC) re-accreditation provided the opportunity to re-evaluate the Taipei American School Strategic Plan. Through a rigorous, two-year self-study and extensive community engagement, we identified the strategic priorities that will shape our next chapter. Today, our successful re-accreditation and Strategic Action Plan provide a clear roadmap for strengthening Taipei American School and advancing our mission into the future. 

Refining Our Strategic Action Plan

Our Strategic Action Plan was developed through an extensive, evidence-based process informed by hundreds of data points and the voices of employees, students, parents and guardians, administrators, and trustees. Rooted in our mission, it provides the framework for our continued growth as a school while strengthening the educational experience and community that define Taipei American School. 

Since its launch in 2024, the Strategic Action Plan has continued to evolve through ongoing reflection, assessment, and community feedback. This refinement has strengthened our priorities, sharpened our goals, and introduced a fifth strategic pillar, Operational Excellence and Institutional Sustainability, recognizing that outstanding learning is supported by strong systems, responsible stewardship, and a sustainable foundation for the future. 

Together, these five strategic pillars guide our work, ensuring that every initiative is aligned with our mission, informed by evidence, and focused on creating lasting impact for our students and community. 

Explore the five strategic pillars below and discover the goals that are shaping the future of Taipei American School.

Strategic Area 1: A Strong Foundation

A Strong Foundation

 

Goal 1:

To strengthen foundational structures to ensure the delivery of an integrated educational experience that achieves the mission and schoolwide learning outcomes.

Aligned to: WASC Category A: Organization for Student Learning

Rationale:

To ensure a strong foundation that allows for the delivery of an integrated educational experience, TAS must implement and evaluate mission-aligned practices. 

  • 1.1 Ensure Strategic Direction and Collective Accountability:
  • 1.2 Optimize Board Governance for Continuous School Improvement:
  • 1.3 Implement Consistent Structures for Community Feedback:

1.1 Ensure Strategic Direction and Collective Accountability:

Ensure TAS is guided by the mission, values, and schoolwide learning outcomes through a clear and comprehensive strategic plan that articulates shared Pre-K-12 goals, establishes the benchmarks for success, and fosters collective responsibility for achievement. 

1.2 Optimize Board Governance for Continuous School Improvement:

Develop and continuously evaluate the governance framework for the TAS Board of Directors, incorporating clearly defined metrics to measure board performance, and enhance the effectiveness of board structure, processes, onboarding, and training to ensure alignment with the school’s evolving needs, as well as clarity of role and responsibilities.

1.3 Implement Consistent Structures for Community Feedback:

Establish and execute a structured system to actively gather, analyze, and respond to community (faculty, staff, students, and parents) feedback.  

  • 1.4 Establish Systems to Enhance the Effectiveness of Professional Growth, Evaluation, and Development Processes for All Employees:
  • 1.5 Attract, Recruit, and Train Faculty, Staff, and Administrators Who Are Able to Execute TAS’s Mission, Values, and Schoolwide Learning Outcomes:

1.4 Establish Systems to Enhance the Effectiveness of Professional Growth, Evaluation, and Development Processes for All Employees:

Foster a culture of continuous improvement and alignment with institutional goals by ensuring all employees are equipped with current skills and knowledge in research-informed practice.  

1.5 Attract, Recruit, and Train Faculty, Staff, and Administrators Who Are Able to Execute TAS’s Mission, Values, and Schoolwide Learning Outcomes:

Implement targeted recruitment strategies, offer comprehensive professional learning, and foster a supportive and inclusive work environment that promotes long-term retention of high-quality faculty, staff,  and administrators aligned with and committed to TAS’s mission, values, schoolwide learning outcomes, and strategic goals.

Strategic Area 2: An Enduring Commitment to Learning

An Enduring Commitment to Learning

 

Goal 2:

To cultivate an enduring commitment to learning across the TAS community through the implementation of current research-based best practices.

Aligned to: WASC Category B: Curriculum, Instruction, and Assessment.

Rationale:

To reinforce an enduring commitment to learning, TAS must adopt a comprehensive approach encompassing the systematic development and documentation of curriculum, coupled with the strategic collection and analysis of student data to enhance teaching effectiveness and inform educational strategies. By investing in robust professional development opportunities aligned with evolving pedagogical landscapes and fostering methods for divisional and cross-divisional collaboration, the aim of Goal 2 is to ensure that all students experience a cohesive, research-driven educational journey supported by the implementation of a vertically and horizontally aligned curriculum and underpinned by data-driven decision-making.

  • 2.1 Ensure the Mission and Schoolwide Learning Outcomes Serve as the Foundation of the Curricular Programs:
  • 2.2 Provide Systematic and Well-Funded Professional Learning in Alignment with Schoolwide Initiatives and Professional Growth and Evaluation:
  • 2.3 Actively Nurture a Consistent Culture of Collaboration:

2.1 Ensure the Mission and Schoolwide Learning Outcomes Serve as the Foundation of the Curricular Programs:

Develop and implement a detailed approach for integrating the mission and SWLOs into all aspects of the program, including scope and sequence documents to outline the progression of skills connected to the Schoolwide Learning Outcome indicators to ensure a consistent, meaningful learning journey for all. 

2.2 Provide Systematic and Well-Funded Professional Learning in Alignment with Schoolwide Initiatives and Professional Growth and Evaluation:

Ensure all faculty are keeping pace with the constantly evolving landscape of pedagogy, promoting greater uniformity in teaching strategies through schoolwide professional learning aligned with TAS initiatives. 

2.3 Actively Nurture a Consistent Culture of Collaboration:

Develop structures to ensure collaboration systematically takes place within divisions and across the school to provide dedicated time for employees to meet, plan, and grow ideas effectively to support achievement of the mission and schoolwide learning outcomes. 

  • 2.4 Implement a Documented, Horizontally and Vertically Aligned Curriculum That Ensures the Achievement of the Mission and Schoolwide Learning Outcomes for all Students:
  • 2.5 Promote Deep, Transfer, and Student-Centered Learning:
  • 2.6 Incorporate Deeper Connections to Taiwan and Taiwanese Culture into Educational Programs:

2.4 Implement a Documented, Horizontally and Vertically Aligned Curriculum That Ensures the Achievement of the Mission and Schoolwide Learning Outcomes for all Students:

Adopt a centralized curriculum repository and consistent PreK-12 standards for each discipline in an effort to enhance alignment. Implement structures to support systematic curricular implementation and oversight to ensure our curriculum provides every student with a consistent, purpose-driven journey aligned with our mission and schoolwide learning outcomes. 

2.5 Promote Deep, Transfer, and Student-Centered Learning:

Develop a more integrated and transdisciplinary approach to learning to support students with making conceptual connections and effectively transferring skills and knowledge across disciplines and outside of the classroom. 

2.6 Incorporate Deeper Connections to Taiwan and Taiwanese Culture into Educational Programs:

Increase opportunities within curricular and extracurricular programs to enhance students’ understanding and awareness of Taiwan and Taiwanese culture, helping to foster a global perspective and respect for cultural diversity among students.

Strategic Area 3: A Supportive, Personalized, and Empowering Student Journey

A Supportive, Personalized, and Empowering Student Journey

 

Goal 3:

To ensure every student’s academic and social-emotional needs are proactively identified and addressed.

Aligned to: WASC Category C: Support for Student Social-Emotional and Academic Growth

Rationale:

To ensure a systematic and schoolwide approach to identifying and supporting the academic and social-emotional needs of each student, TAS should implement the Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS) Framework to establish a cohesive and integrated model that encompasses both academic interventions and social-emotional learning. This approach will be reinforced by targeted professional learning for faculty, as well as clearly defined roles and responsibilities of members within the Student Success Team, both of which will help ensure consistency, appropriate interventions. By doing so, TAS commits to fostering a nurturing environment where all students are empowered and supported to reach their fullest potential. 

  • 3.1 Develop and Execute a Process that Ensures the Systematic Implementation of Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS) across PreK-Grade 12:

3.1 Develop and Execute a Process that Ensures the Systematic Implementation of Multi-Tiered Systems of Support (MTSS) across PreK-Grade 12:

Proactively and consistently identify academic and social-emotional needs, while ensuring students receive timely and appropriate interventions, leading to improved student outcomes. 

  • 3.2 Embed Social-Emotional Learning into All Parts of the Curricular Program:

3.2 Embed Social-Emotional Learning into All Parts of the Curricular Program:

Equip faculty and administrators with the skills and structures to support student well-being consistently and effectively through professional learning and curriculum that on focuses social-emotional learning.

Strategic Area 4: Culture of Safety and Belonging

Culture of Safety and Belonging

 

Goal 4:

To nurture a secure, inclusive, and engaging learning environment.

Aligned to: WASC Category D: School Culture, Child Protection, and Parent / Community Involvement

Rationale:

To nurture a culture of safety and belonging, it is essential that TAS enhances policies and practices related to child protection, digital safety, DEIB, multilingualism, service learning, and parent engagement, as well as providing opportunities for students to develop stronger connections with Taiwan and Taiwanese culture. These measures, collectively, will help to create a secure, inclusive, and engaging learning environment where every community member experiences a sense of belonging.  

  • 4.1 Improve Child Safeguarding Policy and Practice:
  • 4.2 Implement a Comprehensive Digital Safety and Wellness Plan:
  • 4.3 Advance a Culture of Belonging and Social Impact:

4.1 Improve Child Safeguarding Policy and Practice:

Strengthen child protection policies and professional development to ensure comprehensive understanding and compliance across all employees. 

4.2 Implement a Comprehensive Digital Safety and Wellness Plan:

Ensure students are well-equipped with the knowledge and skills needed to navigate digital spaces responsibly and ethically. Educate students, parents, faculty, and staff in developing an understanding of digital security, internet safety protocols, digital footprints, respecting online privacy, and recognizing and responding to cyberbullying. 

4.3 Advance a Culture of Belonging and Social Impact:

Strengthen our community's commitment to our mission and the principles of empathy, inclusivity, and curiosity. 

  • 4.4 Strengthen PreK-12 Practices to Empower Multilingual Learners:
  • 4.5 Increase opportunities to strengthen parent partnerships:

4.4 Strengthen PreK-12 Practices to Empower Multilingual Learners:

Implement a language-additive curriculum and pedagogical practices that develop multilingual learners  

4.5 Increase opportunities to strengthen parent partnerships:

Create opportunities for parents/guardians to partner in bringing the mission, values, and SWLOs to life in our community.

Strategic Area 5: Operational Excellence and Institutional Sustainability

Operational Excellence and Institutional Sustainability

Goal 5:

Operational Excellence and Institutional Sustainability

  • 5.1 Schoolwide Operations:
  • 5.2 Legal Operations:
  • 5.3 Finance Operations:
  • 5.4 IT Operations:
  • 5.5 HR Operations:

5.1 Schoolwide Operations:

To modernize and unify operational departments through digital solutions, workforce empowerment, and sustainability initiatives, ensuring seamless service delivery, data-driven decisions, and stakeholder satisfaction.

5.2 Legal Operations:

Ensure strong and contextually informed legal compliance, legal preparedness, and legal support aligned with all necessary regulatory requirements and the operational needs of TAS. 

5.3 Finance Operations:

Advance efficient, reliable finance services by modernizing systems, developing people, and safeguarding controls, ensuring timely decisions, contained risks, and alignment of resources with TAS’s mission and long-range plans. Strengthen liquidity and long-term capacity. 

5.4 IT Operations:

To strengthen TAS’s technology infrastructure by ensuring business continuity through the identification and elimination of single points of failure, achieving legal compliance with local government regulations, enhancing cybersecurity, expanding internet bandwidth to meet growing institutional demands cost-effectively, and communication continuity. 

5.5 HR Operations:

To build a transparent, fair, and future-ready human resources function that supports staff satisfaction, retention, and operational excellence while addressing legacy inconsistencies and ensuring clear, equitable, and sustainable HR practices. This will enhance TAS's human resource capabilities, support institutional agility, and ensure that HR operations are aligned with best practices and regulatory standards, fostering a positive workplace culture and long-term organizational success. 

  • 5.6 Communications and Marketing:
  • 5.7 Alumni Relations:
  • 5.8 Community Relations:
  • 5.9 Development:
  • 5.10 Enrollment Management:

5.6 Communications and Marketing:

Position TAS as the premier international school in Asia through a unified, audience-centered communications strategy and a distinctive, authentic institutional identity that reflects excellence, innovation, community, and accessibility. Communications will be student-centered, parent-focused, mission-aligned, and rooted in trust and authenticity. 

5.7 Alumni Relations:

Strengthen TAS’s global alumni network as a lifelong, engaged community that enhances the School’s reputation, builds connections worldwide, and creates opportunities for mentorship, partnership, and philanthropy. 

5.8 Community Relations:

Cultivate a cohesive, mission-driven TAS community by strengthening parent, student, and employee engagement through partnership, collaboration, and shared experiences that build trust, belonging, and school pride. 

5.9 Development:

Develop a comprehensive, future-focused fundraising program that secures the resources to sustain TAS’s mission, advance strategic priorities, and ensure long-term financial stability. Fundraising will be transformational, inclusive, and impact-driven.

5.10 Enrollment Management:

Ensure TAS attracts, enrolls, and retains a mission-aligned and diverse student body through a strategic, data-driven enrollment management approach that balances financial sustainability, inclusivity, community fit, and an exceptional family experience from inquiry through graduation.

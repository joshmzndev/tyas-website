TYAS Multilingual Learning Program

The TYAS Multilingual Learning (MLL) Program has been designed and implemented during the 2026-2027 school year at TYAS to support the academic English needs to successfully access and participate in grade-level learning. The program recognizes multilingualism as an asset and values the diverse languages and cultures the students bring to our school community. Therefore, all students will be present within each mainstream class at TYAS.

TYAS has taken the approach of teaching content and language within an integrated teaching framework. The teachers use Content and Language Integrated Learning (CLIL) approaches to simultaneously develop students’ academic language while learning the subject content.

The MLL program at TYAS has introduced co-teaching in Grades 1-8. This approach allows students to receive classroom-based support by following WIDA’s collaborative co-teaching model. Homeroom teachers are supported by learning support teachers in Grades 1-5, while Grades 6-8 receive support from MLL teachers. In addition to support provided during mainstream classes, students who require additional targeted language development in Elementary and Middle School may also participate in Period 9 language classes. These classes provide students with the opportunity to strengthen their academic English and develop language skills to access grade-level learning.

In Grades 9-12, the program shifts towards greater academic independence and university readiness. Classroom-based co-teaching is no longer provided, yet students requiring additional English language development may enroll in an Academic English elective, designed to strengthen the language skills needed for success in high school and prepare them for future academic study.

The goal of the TYAS Multilingual Learner Program is to ensure that multilingual learners have the same opportunities and equitable access to rigorous learning while still developing their academic language, confidence, and independence to thrive academically and as active members of the school community.

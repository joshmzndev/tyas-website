Our Commitment

Safeguarding
at TYAS

At Taoyuan American School (TYAS), safeguarding means ensuring that every student feels safe—physically, emotionally, and socially. We believe that a secure and caring environment allows students to thrive, grow, and reach their full potential.

Our mission guides our safeguarding commitment: Our school is committed to fostering holistic development and preparing students for lifelong success. Our mission is to provide a comprehensive education that nurtures academic accomplishment while fostering the social and emotional well-being of each learner. We aim to cultivate diligent, resilient, and adaptable global citizens and equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and mindset to pursue lifelong learning and personal fulfillment.

By embedding safeguarding into our values and mission, TYAS commits to
  • Promoting a safe learning environment where students feel valued and respected.
  • Providing clear structures and systems to respond swiftly to concerns.
  • Empowering students with the knowledge and confidence to protect themselves and others.
  • Supporting staff and families in understanding their role in safeguarding every child.

At TYAS, safeguarding is at the heart of who we are. Together, we create a community where every student can feel safe, supported, and inspired to learn.

Child Protection

Our Approach to
Child Protection

TYAS is located in Taiwan, where the school complies with and supports child protection as the R.O.C. government requires.

TYAS also recognizes internationally accepted safeguarding expectations, including those reflected in the International Taskforce on Child Protection (ITFCP) and the International Centre for Missing & Exploited Children (ICMEC), while remaining cognizant of the legal context of Taiwan.

Based on the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (1989)
  • Every TYAS child has the right to be safe from harm and those entrusted with the care of children are required to create and provide a safe environment for them.
  • Every TYAS child has the right to be protected from physical or mental violence, injury or abuse, neglect or negligent treatment, maltreatment or exploitation, including sexual abuse.
  • A safe TYAS child is not exposed to any form of discipline involving violence.
  • A safe TYAS child has the freedom of expression and religion.
Anti-Bullying

Anti-Bullying
at TYAS

At TYAS, we believe every child deserves to learn and grow in a safe, respectful, and supportive environment. Bullying, harassment, and discrimination—whether physical, verbal, social, or online—have no place in our community.

Our Anti-Bullying Policy makes it clear: we will not tolerate any behavior that harms the dignity or well-being of others. This includes bullying, harassment, sexual harassment, and discrimination based on race, gender, identity, religion, or ability.

To ensure our entire community understands this commitment, both students and parents sign an Anti-Bullying Policy Receipt each year. By signing, families affirm that they understand that TYAS has zero tolerance for bullying and agree to work in partnership with the school to maintain a safe and respectful environment.

Together, we are building a culture of kindness, respect, and inclusion where every student feels safe, supported, and empowered to succeed.

Mental Health

Emotional
Well-Being

At TYAS, we believe that a student's emotional health is just as important as their academic success. That's why we have dedicated policies and supports in place for mental health and suicide prevention.

Our Emotional Well-Being Specialist works closely with students, teachers, and families to provide early support, counseling, and resources for those who may be struggling.

Whether it's managing stress, building resilience, or addressing more serious concerns, our goal is to ensure every student has a safe space to be heard and supported.

TYAS also integrates emotional well-being into the classroom experience through advisory programs, workshops, and awareness activities. When needed, we connect families with external counseling and community resources to ensure students have access to the care they need.

By combining early identification, direct support, and a culture of care, TYAS is committed to helping every child thrive—academically, socially, and emotionally.

Safe Hiring

Safe Hiring &
Staff Monitoring

At TYAS, your child's safety is our top priority. All staff members undergo strict police background and reference checks, and we verify their qualifications before they begin work. Every teacher and staff member completes child protection training and signs a code of conduct that clearly defines professional expectations.

We also renew police background checks every two years and provide ongoing safeguarding training to ensure our standards remain strong. With these measures in place, families can be confident that TYAS is committed to maintaining a safe, supportive, and trustworthy environment for every student.

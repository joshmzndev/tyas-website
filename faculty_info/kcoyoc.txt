Karina holds a Bachelor's degree in Chemistry Education and a Master's degree in International Health from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University in Taiwan. Her educational journey and teaching experiences have given her an international perspective on science education. She has several years of experience teaching secondary science in Belize and has additional teaching experience in Taiwan. Karina is passionate about creating a supportive and engaging learning environment where students develop curiosity, resilience, and confidence through scientific inquiry. She believes the best learning happens when students ask meaningful questions, think critically, collaborate with others, and apply science to better understand the world around them. Outside of school, Karina enjoys exploring nature through hiking and cycling, gardening, traveling, and challenging herself by learning new hobbies and skills.

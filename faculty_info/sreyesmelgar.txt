Jeffrey Sebastian also known as Mr. Bear brings a unique interdisciplinary background that bridges engineering, education, multimedia design, and emerging technologies. He holds a Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering and pursued doctoral research in the same field at Yuan Ze University. His academic and professional journey is complemented by a Bachelor's degree in Multimedia Design, a robotics patent, and professional certifications in artificial intelligence, including Google AI credentials.

With experience spanning higher education, STEAM education, curriculum development, digital innovation, and educational leadership, Jeffrey has served as an adjunct professor, Digital Development Director, multimedia producer, and international educator. He has designed and delivered engaging learning experiences across robotics, programming, engineering, artificial intelligence, multimedia production, and project-based learning, helping students develop the creativity, critical thinking, and adaptability needed for the future.

Jeffrey's teaching philosophy is centered on making complex ideas approachable through hands-on exploration, real-world applications, and meaningful technology integration. He believes that education should inspire curiosity, foster innovation, and empower students to become confident problem-solvers while embracing lifelong learning. As a multilingual educator, he values building inclusive learning environments that encourage collaboration and global perspectives. Beyond the classroom, Jeffrey enjoys designing educational resources, creating digital media, exploring emerging technologies, and developing innovative learning experiences. He is passionate about robotics, artificial intelligence, graphic design, photography, music, and game design, continually seeking new ways to combine creativity with technology to inspire the next generation of learners.

Nathaniel Lewis is a graduate of the University of California: Davis and received his teaching accreditation at California State University: Bakersfield. He has taught high school in both the United States and Taiwan, with two decades of experience. His approach to teaching has always been to help students become confident in themselves and what they're capable of accomplishing through mentorship and guidance. He firmly believes in the power of language and learning from past experiences, whether they be yours or by empathizing with others'. In his free time, he enjoys puzzles, bicycling, literature and music.

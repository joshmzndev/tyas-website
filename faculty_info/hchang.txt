Currently serving as a Learning Support Teacher at Taoyuan American School, Hui-Ya has seven years of classroom experience teaching multilingual learners from kindergarten through senior high school. She holds a degree in Professional Teaching, a TESOL Certificate, and a TEYL certification. With a particular interest in multilingual education, she enjoys finding creative ways to make lessons more accessible and meaningful for students with different English-language abilities and learning needs. Passionate about inclusive education, Hui-Ya believes that every student learns differently and strives to create a positive learning environment where students can build confidence, grow, and become more independent learners.
Fluent in English, Mandarin Chinese, and Filipino, Hui-Ya appreciates connecting with people from diverse backgrounds. Outside of school, she enjoys traveling, exploring new places and cultures, staying active, and continuously learning new skills.

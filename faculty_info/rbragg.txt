Mr. Bragg is a licensed educator of British and Filipino heritage. Raised in the United Kingdom, he later spent several years living and teaching in the Philippines. His multicultural background has shaped his global outlook and his appreciation for different languages and cultures. He has also studied Chinese reading and writing and is working on his HSK2 certificate. 

Mr. Bragg is committed to creating an inclusive classroom where every student feels respected, represented, and valued. He strongly believes that our differences make each of us unique and special, and that learning and working together within diverse communities helps everyone grow. He encourages his students to listen to different perspectives, support one another, and approach the world with curiosity, kindness, and respect.

Outside the classroom, Mr. Bragg enjoys jogging, swimming, and meditating in nature. These activities help him maintain a balanced and mindful approach to both teaching and everyday life.

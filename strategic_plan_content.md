Our Strategic Plan

Aligned and Integrated with our Five Year Plan and CIS and WASC Accreditation

TYAS’s strategic priorities are guided by the school’s Five-Year Strategic Plan and further informed and refined by feedback from the 2025 CIS/WASC Preparatory Engagement. Together, these have helped shape the school’s focus on continuous improvement in areas such as governance and leadership, curriculum and assessment, multilingual learning, student well-being and safeguarding, professional growth, and organizational sustainability.

The Strategic Plan serves as the school’s overarching long-term direction, while the accreditation process provides external feedback, evidence, and reflection that help strengthen priorities, monitor progress, and guide ongoing school improvement.

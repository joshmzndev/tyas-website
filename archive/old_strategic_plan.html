5-year Strategic Plan (2024-2029)

TYAS' five-year strategic plan focuses on strengthening our educational programs, expanding international collaborations, and enhancing student outcomes in alignment with our vision to prepare students for the future. This plan underscores our commitment to fostering a multicultural, globally aware learning environment, where students can thrive both academically and socially.

Major Future Development Projects 2025 and Beyond

In line with the guiding principles of TYAS' Five-Year Strategic Plan: There are several major developments that will take place in the coming years:
1. Campus Estate and Infrastructure Developments
TYAS is actively considering campus expansion and infrastructure renovation plans to further enhance overall educational development, improve facilities and equipment, and elevate teaching quality, in alignment with the objectives and action plans set in the five-year strategic plan.
With the full support of the Board, a due diligence process is currently underway with professional legal and financial advisors to ensure sustainability and risk control. This significant renovation plan will be a key topic of internal discussions and decision-making over the next five years. It will require the support of relevant stakeholders and communication with education authorities. This major initiative will be one of the most critical strategic decisions for TYAS in the coming years.
2. Dual Accreditation From the CIS and WASC
Actively obtaining accreditation from both CIS and WASC is a key step for TYAS in achieving teaching excellence, providing students with holistic educational opportunities, and developing them into globally-minded leaders. Successfully attaining accreditation is an important milestone, and TYAS is committed to fully meeting its goals throughout the entire accreditation cycle. This also represents a shared commitment and collective effort from all key stakeholders of the School.

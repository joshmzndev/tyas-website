About the
Principal

  1. Dr. Fujiing Shiue joined Taoyuan American School (TYAS) in August 2024.
     

    He obtained his Ph.D. in Accounting from the George Washington University, preceded by a Master of International Business from the same esteemed institution. His academic journey began at National Taiwan University where he earned a Bachelor of Business Administration. This strong academic foundation has been imperative in shaping his career in both educational and administrative capacities.
     

    Throughout his professional tenure, Dr. Shiue has held many different leadership roles that demonstrate his commitment to educational excellence and his adeptness in administration. He has notably served as the President and Dean of Academic Affairs at National Taipei University, where he was instrumental in enhancing academic programs and expanding educational opportunities. Dr. Shiue’s leadership extended to influential roles in major educational foundations, including as Chairman of the Accounting Research and Development Foundation, President of the Taiwan Accounting Association, and independent board members of listing companies. These positions, combined with his significant experiences in international accreditation for specialized university disciplines, as well as participating in overall institutional and curriculum evaluation, have equipped him with a deep understanding of the challenges and opportunities in leading an international educational institution like TYAS.

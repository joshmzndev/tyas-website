# 1\. Safeguarding at TYAS

At Taoyuan American School, the safety, dignity and well-being of every student are our highest priorities. We are committed to providing a safe, caring, inclusive and respectful environment in which every student feels protected, heard and able to seek help.

Safeguarding is everyone’s responsibility. All members of our community are encouraged to speak up whenever they are worried about their own safety or well-being or are concerned about another student. You do not need to be certain that something is wrong before sharing a concern.

# 2\. Report a Safeguarding Concern

If you are worried about your own safety or well-being, or you are concerned about another student, please tell us. You may speak to any trusted adult at school or use one of the reporting options below.

Reports are reviewed by authorized school personnel and handled as privately as possible. Information may need to be shared with people who can help protect a student or when reporting is required by law. TYAS does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Important

# 3\. Immediate and Emergency Help

Use the following services when urgent help is needed outside school hours or when waiting for a school response could place someone at risk.

### Immediate danger or a crime in progress \- Police 110

Call 110 when someone is in immediate danger, violence is occurring, or urgent police assistance is required.

### Child protection, domestic violence, sexual assault or sexual harassment \- Protection Hotline 113

The 113 Protection Hotline provides free, 24-hour support, consultation and reporting services. Interpretation support is available in several languages, including English. If there is immediate danger, call 110 first.

### Medical emergency or fire \- 119

Call 119 when urgent medical assistance, an ambulance or fire service is required.

### Emotional crisis or suicide-prevention support \- 1925

Call 1925 for suicide-prevention and emotional-crisis support. If there is an immediate threat to life, call 119 or 110 and remain with the person when it is safe to do so.

# 5\. What Happens After a Concern Is Reported?

Every concern is taken seriously. The exact response will depend on the information received, the student’s needs and any legal reporting requirements.

#### **VISUAL PLACEMENT \- FIVE-STEP RESPONSE GRAPHIC**

*Place a simple vertical or horizontal flowchart here using five numbered icons: 1\) Listen and Record, 2\) Check Immediate Safety, 3\) Assess and Report, 4\) Provide Support, and 5\) Follow Up. On mobile devices, the steps should stack vertically. Suggested alternative text: “Five-step TYAS safeguarding response: listen and record, check safety, assess and report, provide support, and follow up.”*

### 1\. We listen and record

An authorized member of staff records the information carefully and avoids asking unnecessary or leading questions.

### 2\. We check immediate safety

The safeguarding team considers whether anyone needs urgent medical care, supervision, separation, emergency assistance or another immediate protective measure.

### 3\. We assess and report

The concern is reviewed by the appropriate safeguarding personnel. When required, TYAS reports the matter to the appropriate external authority within the legally required timeframe. A person reporting a concern is not expected to investigate or prove what happened.

### 4\. We provide support

The school considers appropriate support for the student and others affected. This may include a trusted-adult check-in, counseling, health support, supervision changes, safety planning or referral to an external service.

### 5\. We follow up

The school records decisions, monitors safety and reviews whether further action or support is needed. Safeguarding action and disciplinary action are considered separately. Privacy requirements may prevent the school from sharing another person’s confidential information or disciplinary outcome.

## Our commitments

We will take concerns seriously.  
We will respond as promptly as possible.

We will share information only when necessary to protect students, provide support or meet legal responsibilities.

We will continue to support students after the initial report.

# Safeguarding Page Blueprint (modeled on Taipei American School)

A reference for building a school **Safeguarding** page with the same structure, content types, and visual style as TAS's page (`tas.edu.tw/about/safeguarding`, reviewed Oct 20, 2026). Content below is summarized and paraphrased so you can adapt it to your own school; replace everything in `[brackets]`.

---

## 1. Page at a glance

| Item | TAS approach |
|---|---|
| Location in site | About Us → Safeguarding (also in footer "Useful Links" and the "How Do I?" quick links) |
| Breadcrumb | Home › About Us › Safeguarding |
| Purpose | Reassure prospective families, give current families/staff/students a fast way to report a concern, and act as a hub for all child-protection-related policies |
| Tone | Warm, values-led, confident; "safeguarding is everyone's responsibility" |
| Platform | Finalsite CMS (any CMS works) |

## 2. Section-by-section structure (top to bottom)

### 2.1 Hero
- Full-width photo of smiling students (TAS uses older students in blazers holding red apples).
- Overlaid site header: logo left, utility nav (Join Our School button, Zones dropdown, Calendars, Search) and main nav.
- Below the hero: a dark navy band with the **section sub-navigation** (all "About Us" child pages, current page highlighted in gold/orange with underline).

### 2.2 Page title block
- Small brand icon (leaf/petal shape in blue + gold) beside the H1.
- **H1:** "Safeguarding Our Students"
- **H2 tagline:** mascot-based line — e.g. "For Every [Mascot]: Safe, Valued, and Ready to Learn"
- **H3 belief statement (uppercase, blue):** the school believes a safe, caring, inclusive environment is the foundation for students to thrive emotionally, socially, and academically.

### 2.3 Image + callout card #1
- Large photo (young students with a teacher, hands raised) on the left ~60%.
- Overlapping translucent purple card on the right with:
  - Serif heading: learning thrives in a physically and emotionally safe environment.
  - Body line: safeguarding is everyone's responsibility.

### 2.4 "Why Safeguarding Matters at [School]" — two columns
**Left column (paragraphs, key phrases bolded):**
- Student safety and wellbeing is **our highest priority**; children learn best when secure, respected, and empowered.
- The school **actively fosters a culture in which all students are protected from harm, abuse, neglect, or exploitation**.
- It follows global best practice, adapted to the local and international community context.

**Right column — "OUR COMMITMENTS & PRINCIPLES" (uppercase blue H3, bulleted):**
1. Every child has the right to feel safe and be safe.
2. Prevent harm, respond swiftly to concerns, support recovery and growth.
3. Uphold the highest standards of professional conduct.
4. Inclusive: applies to all students regardless of age, ability, background, gender identity, culture, or faith.
5. Partner with families and the wider community to build a protective network.

### 2.5 "For Urgent Safeguarding Assistance" — navy feature panel
Dark navy box, white text, megaphone icon before the H2. Two columns:

**Left:**
- Serif H4: "Are you a current parent, student, or employee looking for immediate assistance in reporting a safeguarding issue?"
- Thank-you line ("…health and well-being of our [Mascots]").
- Large link with circular blue arrow button: **Contact the Schoolwide Safeguarding Lead, [Name]** → `mailto:[safeguarding email]`
- Serif H4: "For additional information on our Safeguarding Policies, please refer to the [Parent Portal]:" (linked)
- Bulleted resource links (each split by division where applicable):
  - PDFs of all Safeguarding Policies (Lower / Middle / Upper School)
  - Divisional Handbooks (Lower / Middle / Upper School)
  - Safeguarding for Visitors to Campus
  - Our Safeguarding Partners
  - Safeguarding Team (mailto link)
  - Safeguarding & Well-being Contacts (Lower / Middle / Upper School)

**Right:** headshot of the Safeguarding Lead, then name (bold), title "Schoolwide Safeguarding Lead", and email link.

Decorative brand petal in the bottom-left corner of the panel.

> TAS uses a dedicated shared inbox (`safe@…`) — recommended so reports don't depend on one person's mailbox.

### 2.6 "What We Do – Prevention & Protection" — 2×2 grid of lists
| Card (blue uppercase H3) | Bullet points |
|---|---|
| **Recruitment and Training** | Screening, background checks, and safeguarding training before anyone works at the school · Background checks renewed every 3 years · Annual training for all employees, contract staff, and on-campus vendors |
| **Safe Campus & Systems** | Secure access, visitor protocols, ID checks so every adult is identifiable · CCTV in public spaces where appropriate · Risk assessments for field trips, off-campus activities, bus transport · Codes of conduct for staff, students, and all adults · Annual leadership review of the Child Protection Policy |
| **Student Education & Empowerment** | Age-appropriate lessons on personal safety, digital well-being, respectful relationships, bullying prevention · Wellbeing/SEL woven into curriculum · Student voice: speak up, ask, seek help |
| **Responses & Support** | Clear procedures to receive, record, and respond to concerns/disclosures · Designated Safeguarding Leads guide students, families, staff · Support and referral for students who have experienced harm |

### 2.7 Image + callout card #2 (mirrored)
- Purple card on the **left**, photo on the right (parent with young child at school drop-off).
- Serif heading: a safe, supportive environment is the foundation all students deserve — the foundation of the mission and the number one priority.
- Body: commitment to model ethical behavior per the school's core values (TAS: honesty, respect, responsibility, kindness, courage).

### 2.8 "Our Roles & Responsibilities" — four short cards
- **Safeguarding Team** — Safeguarding Lead, divisional principals, counselors, psychologists, campus security, HR, and others.
- **All Teachers & Adults on Campus** — every employee is a mandatory reporter and upholds the safeguarding culture.
- **Parents & Caregivers** — partners: model safe interactions, reinforce expectations, collaborate when concerns arise.
- **Students** — look after their own and peers' wellbeing; speak up if unsafe; support one another; act with respect.

### 2.9 "Reporting & Response Process for Safeguarding Concerns"
Intro: "If you have a concern about a child's safety or wellbeing, please:"
1. Immediately contact the Safeguarding Lead or a Safeguarding Team member.
2. The school assesses, records, and responds per policy, focused on the student's wellbeing.
3. Confidentiality is kept as far as possible, but information may be shared with agencies when a student is at risk, per [local law].

### 2.10 "Partnering with Parents & Community"
Intro: safeguarding is shared; the community is invited to:
- Understand and support policies and codes of conduct.
- Respect campus rules, visitor protocols, and digital-safety norms.
- Tell the school about family/home changes that may affect a student.
- Work with staff when a concern arises.
- Attend parent workshops (wellbeing, online safety, bullying prevention).

### 2.11 "Important Safeguarding Resources" — tabbed panel
Horizontal tabs (light grey, bold label; collapses to accordion on mobile). Each tab = one policy summary with optional PDF link.

| Tab | What TAS includes (summary) |
|---|---|
| **Child Protection Policy** | Based on national child-protection law + US best practice + expert guidance; employees must report suspected abuse immediately to a supervisor, counselor, or principal; anyone may report to any employee or directly to authorities; references the suicide prevention policy. |
| **Protocol for Reporting** | Good-faith-suspicion standard; chain of command: employee → principal → Head of School + COO; school assesses need for immediate intervention; Compliance/Regulatory Manager reports to authorities. List the authorities (TAS: city Domestic Violence & Sexual Assault Center, Ministry of Education, Ministry of Health and Welfare). |
| **Suicide Prevention Policy** | Board-adopted; curriculum on warning signs, coping, help-seeking (by division: guidance lessons / homeroom & health / advisory & health); help-seeking promoted schoolwide; risk assessment by psychologist/counselor; external resource directory with emails/phones (community counseling center, 24-hr English line, national hotline); "safety comes first" over confidentiality. |
| **Harassment & Bullying Policy** | Link to full PreK–12 policy PDF; right to participate without harassment, hazing, sexual harassment, bullying, cyberbullying, or retaliation — on or off campus; sub-sections defining Hazing & Bullying, Harassment, Sexual Harassment (verbal / nonverbal / physical / sexual examples), and how to report. |
| **Transgender & Gender Nonconforming Students Policy** | Commitment to a safe, welcoming, inclusive environment; whom students can talk to if questioning or transitioning. |
| **Photography, Video & Social Media Guidelines** | Framework for safe use of images; visitors discouraged from photographing on campus; how to request image removal (communications email). |
| **Privacy Policy** | How personal information is protected and used. |
| **Non-Discrimination Policy** | Admissions and programs open regardless of race, color, national/ethnic origin, etc. |
| **Acceptable Use Policies** | Digital citizenship; network account use; caution posting personal info online. |

> Write your own policy text and have it reviewed against your jurisdiction's law — don't copy another school's policies.

### 2.12 "Belonging in Action" — news cards
Four cards pulled from the news feed (image, title, "Read More"). TAS examples: World Kindness Day, student-led inclusion on the basketball court, student clubs at Pride, middle school camp. Tag safeguarding/belonging stories so this feed populates automatically.

### 2.13 "Discover More" CTA band
Short line inviting prospective families, followed by three image tiles: **Our Community**, **Our Campus**, **Our Academics**.

### 2.14 Footer
Logo + one-sentence school description; accreditation logos (WASC, IB, AP); contact (address, phone, Google Maps link); Useful Links (Mission & Values, Leadership, **Safeguarding**, Careers, Mobile App); social icons; Sitemap / Website Privacy / Accessibility / Non-Discrimination links. Floating "Highlights" side tab (news, calendar, social feed) and Back-to-top button.

## 3. Visual design system

### Colors
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy (primary) | `#252C6A` (rgb 37,44,106) | H1/H2 text, nav bar, urgent-assistance panel |
| Bright blue | `#0071CE` (rgb 0,113,206) | Uppercase H3 subheads, arrow buttons |
| Purple callout | ~`#4B4790` at ~60% opacity (renders lavender) | Overlapping image cards |
| Gold/orange accent | ~`#F5A623` | Active nav underline, brand petal |
| Body text | `#3D4047` (rgb 61,64,71) | Paragraphs, lists |
| Tab background | `rgba(35,35,39,0.1)` | Inactive tabs |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Page background, text on navy |

### Typography
| Element | Font | Size / weight |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Playfair Display (serif) | ~51px / 700, navy |
| H2 | Playfair Display | ~30px / 700, navy |
| H3 | Montserrat (sans) | ~19px / 600, UPPERCASE, bright blue |
| H4 / callout headings | Playfair Display | ~21px / 700 (white on dark) |
| Body, lists, tabs | Montserrat | 16px / 400 (tabs 600) |
| Nav / utility labels | Montserrat, uppercase, letter-spaced | small |

Both fonts are on Google Fonts.

### Layout patterns
- Content width ~1100–1200px, centered; generous vertical whitespace between sections.
- Alternating **image + overlapping callout card** blocks (card offset, drop shadow) to break up text.
- Two-column text sections on desktop, stacking on mobile.
- One high-contrast navy panel reserved for the urgent "get help" content so it stands out.
- Icons: megaphone for urgent help; circular arrow buttons on primary links; brand petal motif as decoration.
- Sticky top nav on scroll (compact navy bar with logo, Join button, main nav).

## 4. Assets to prepare
- Hero photo (students), 2 lifestyle photos for callout cards (classroom; family drop-off), Safeguarding Lead headshot.
- Brand icon/petal SVG, megaphone icon, arrow icon.
- Policy PDFs (by division), divisional handbooks, visitor safeguarding guide, partners list, divisional contact pages.
- Safeguarding inbox (e.g., `safeguarding@[school]`), plus local helplines and authority names for your jurisdiction.
- 4 news stories tagged "belonging"/"safeguarding".

## 5. Accessibility & build notes
- Single H1; logical H2 → H3 order; uppercase via CSS, not typed caps.
- Alt text on all photos (TAS's content images lack it — improve on this).
- Tabs should use proper ARIA (`role="tablist"`, `tab`, `tabpanel`) and become an accordion on small screens.
- Make the urgent-help email/phone visible without scrolling far on mobile; consider a phone number alongside email.
- Ensure white-on-lavender card text meets contrast (TAS's translucent cards are borderline — use a more opaque purple).
- Add a "Skip to main content" link and a "Last reviewed [date]" note for policies.

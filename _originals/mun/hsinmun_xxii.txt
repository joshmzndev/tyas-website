TYAS at HSINMUN XXII
From October 16 to 18, 2026, 13 TYAS students took part in HSINMUN XXII, representing countries across nine committees, including the General Assembly, the Security Council, the Human Rights Council, UNDP, and the Environment Committee. Delegates researched their countries’ positions, wrote and delivered opening speeches, and spent three days debating issues such as the crisis in Haiti, governance of the Arctic, rural poverty, and water scarcity. They gave speeches, asked points of information, and proposed amendments alongside students from schools across Taiwan. Congratulations to all our delegates on their preparation and effort, and thank you to the HSINMUN organizers for hosting such a rewarding conference.
